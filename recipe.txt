Simple Pilau Recipe

Ingredients:
- 2 cups basmati rice
- 1 tablespoon oil or ghee
- 1 onion, finely sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon ginger, grated
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 3 cardamom pods
- 4 cloves
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1 teaspoon curry powder or pilau spice mix
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
- 1 1/2 cups stock or water
- 1/2 cup peas or mixed vegetables (optional)
- Salt, to taste
- Fresh coriander or parsley, to garnish

Method:
1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, then drain.
2. Heat the oil or ghee in a pot over medium heat. Fry the onion until soft and golden.
3. Add the garlic, ginger, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, bay leaf, and cumin. Stir for 30 seconds.
4. Mix in the curry powder and turmeric, then add the rice and stir to coat.
5. Pour in the stock or water and add salt. Add peas or vegetables if using.
6. Bring to a boil, cover, and reduce the heat to low. Cook for 15-18 minutes until the rice is tender and fluffy.
7. Let it sit covered for 5 minutes, then fluff gently with a fork.
8. Garnish with fresh coriander or parsley and serve warm.

Serves: 2-3
